Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

Animosaic is an animal jigsaw puzzle game for children, published by Dmytro Zinenko, Ukraine (“we”). This policy explains what data the app handles. In short: the app has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics, and it does not ask for a name, e-mail, location, photos, contacts or any other personal information.

Data that stays on your device

Game progress (which pictures are completed), an unfinished puzzle and settings (sound, music, hint, language) are stored only on the device. We cannot see them. They are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Purchases

The full version is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Payment is processed by Google; we never receive card or payment details. To confirm and restore purchases the app uses RevenueCat, Inc. (revenuecat.com). RevenueCat receives a random anonymous app user ID created on the device, purchase information (product, date, store receipt), and basic technical data such as app version, operating system version and country/currency for prices. This data is not linked to your name or e-mail by us and is used only to unlock and restore the full version. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy and Google’s privacy policy.

Downloading pictures

Some pictures are downloaded from our content server at cdn.animosaic.app, hosted by Cloudflare, Inc. As with any internet request, Cloudflare processes the device’s IP address and request details to deliver the files and protect the service. We do not use this to identify you. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

Children

The app is designed for children and their parents. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. There are no ads, no social features, no chat and no links out of the app that a child can open without help: purchases and links are behind a parental check (a simple question for an adult).

What we do not do

  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking.
  • No selling or sharing of data with third parties beyond the services named above, which act only to provide the app’s features.

Your rights

Because we do not hold personal data about you, there is usually nothing for us to access or delete. You can delete local data by uninstalling the app. To ask about purchase data held by RevenueCat, or about anything in this policy, contact us at pirate.dmitry@gmail.com; we will reply within 30 days. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU or the Law of Ukraine “On Personal Data Protection”), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data and to complain to a data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page and change the effective date above.
